## Overview

**ERASURE** offers fully extensible built-in components, allowing users to define custom unlearning techniques, integrate custom and synthetic datasets, implement tailored evaluation metrics, and seamlessly work with state-of-the-art machine learning models.

Machine Unlearning (MU) enables selective removal of learned information from AI models without full retraining, addressing critical needs in:
- **Data Privacy** (GDPR Right to be Forgotten)
- **Bias Mitigation**
- **Regulatory Compliance** (AI Act, CCPA)
