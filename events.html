Workshop Series

2025--2026

WIPE-OUT @ ECML-PKDD

Workshop on Innovations, Privacy-preservation, and Evaluations Of machine Unlearning Techniques. Focusing on selective data removal from AI models for privacy, bias mitigation, and regulatory compliance.

2024

DELTA @ KDD

Workshop on Discovering Drift Phenomena in Evolving Data Landscapes at KDD 2024. Covering drift detection, data streams, and adaptive machine learning.

2020--2023

BIAS @ ECIR

International Workshop on Algorithmic Bias in Search and Recommendation, co-located with ECIR. Four successful editions addressing fairness, transparency, and social impacts of search and recommendation systems.

2017--2019

SoMePeAS / SoAPS @ ECIR

Workshops on Social Media for Personalization and Search, and Social Aspects in Personalization and Search. Three editions exploring social signals for improved personalization and information retrieval.

2018

SIR @ CIKM

Workshop on Social Interaction-based Recommendation at CIKM 2018. Extending the classic notion of social recommendation by leveraging diverse social interaction data.

2016--2017

SERecSys @ ICDM

Workshop on Semantics-Enabled Recommender Systems. Two editions at ICDM exploring semantic knowledge to enrich recommendation processes.
