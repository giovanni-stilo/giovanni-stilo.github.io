We are delighted to announce that **Professor Giovanni Stilo** has been recognized as one of the **Top 500 Most Influential Italians in Artificial Intelligence (AI)**.

Professor Stilo has made significant contributions to AI through his research in Machine Learning, Explainable AI, Algorithmic Fairness, and Data Mining. His expertise spans Graph Neural Networks, Anomaly Detection, Temporal Mining, Social Network Analysis, Network Medicine, and Semantic Recommendation Systems.
