We are happy to announce that our proposal on **Workshop on Machine Unlearning and Privacy Preservation (WIPE-OUT 2026)** has been accepted as a workshop at ECML-PKDD 2026, taking place on September 7, 2026 in Naples, Italy.

The workshop focuses on Machine Unlearning (MU), enabling selective removal of learned information from AI models without full retraining, addressing data privacy, bias mitigation, and regulatory compliance (GDPR, AI Act, CCPA, Bill C-27).

Topics span four areas: Foundations, Applications, Evaluation and Benchmarking, and Implications.
