**Workshop at the 41st European Conference on Information Retrieval (ECIR 2019)**, April 14, 2019.

Social media platforms have become powerful tools to collect user preferences and get to know them more. Being able to exploit social media to mine user behavior and extract additional information leads to improvements in the accuracy of personalization and search technologies, and to better targeted services.

This workshop aimed to collect novel ideas in this field and to provide a common ground for researchers working in this area.
