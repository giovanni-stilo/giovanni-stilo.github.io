**Tutorial at ACM Conference on Information & Knowledge Management (CIKM 2020)**, held online on October 19--20, 2020 (original host city: Galway, Ireland).

This tutorial addressed challenges and solutions to the student dropout prediction problem in online courses. It covered data collection, feature engineering, predictive modeling, and evaluation strategies for identifying students at risk of dropping out.

## Presenters

- Bardh Prenkaj
- Giovanni Stilo
- Lorenzo Madeddu
