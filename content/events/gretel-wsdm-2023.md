**Demo at WSDM 2023: The 16th ACM International Conference on Web Search and Data Mining**, March 2, 2023.

This demo discussed the challenges of interpreting Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) in social networks and introduced Graph Counterfactual Explanation (GCE) methods as a solution. The GRETEL framework was presented as a modular tool for developing and evaluating GCE methods.

- Mario Alfonso Prado-Romero
- Bardh Prenkaj
- Giovanni Stilo
