The **BIAS** workshop series (2020--2023) addressed algorithmic bias and fairness in search and recommendation systems. Four successful editions co-located with the European Conference on Information Retrieval (ECIR).

## Editions

- **BIAS 2023** -- Dublin, Ireland (ECIR 2023)
- **BIAS 2022** -- Stavanger, Norway (ECIR 2022)
- **BIAS 2021** -- Virtual (ECIR 2021)
- **BIAS 2020** -- Lisbon, Portugal (ECIR 2020)

## Organizers

- Ludovico Boratto
- Stefano Faralli
- Mirko Marras
- Giovanni Stilo

All editions published proceedings in Springer CCIS and LNCS.
