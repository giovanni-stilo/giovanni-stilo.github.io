**Workshop at the 39th European Conference on Information Retrieval (ECIR 2017)**, April 9, 2017.

Social media platforms have become powerful tools to collect the preferences of users and get to know them more. Being able to exploit social media to mine user behavior and extract additional information leads to improvements in the accuracy of personalization and search technologies, and to better targeted services to the users.

This workshop aimed to collect novel ideas in this field and to provide a common ground for researchers working in this area.
