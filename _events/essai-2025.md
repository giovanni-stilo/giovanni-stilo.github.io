**Course at the 3rd European Summer School on Artificial Intelligence (ESSAI 2025)**, June 30 -- July 4, 2025.

This PhD course explored Machine Unlearning, covering its theoretical foundations, state-of-the-art techniques, evaluation metrics, and practical hands-on benchmarking to efficiently "forget" specific training data without full model retraining.

## Instructors

- Flavio Giobergia (Polytechnic of Turin)
- Giovanni Stilo (Luiss University of Rome)

## Topics Covered

- Theoretical foundations of machine unlearning
- State-of-the-art unlearning techniques
- Evaluation metrics and benchmarking
- Hands-on practical sessions with the ERASURE framework
