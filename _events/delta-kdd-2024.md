The **DELTA 2024** workshop at KDD 2024 addressed cutting-edge challenges in drift detection, data streams, and adaptive machine learning, held on **August 26, 2024 in Barcelona, Spain**.

The workshop explored discovering drift phenomena in evolving data landscapes, covering concept drift detection, data stream mining, and adaptive learning methods.

## Topics of Interest

- Concept drift detection and adaptation
- Data stream mining and real-time analytics
- Evolving data distributions in production ML systems
- Temporal dynamics in graph data
- Drift-aware recommender systems
