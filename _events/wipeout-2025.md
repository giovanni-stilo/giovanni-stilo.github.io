The **Workshop on Innovations, Privacy-preservation, and Evaluations Of machine Unlearning Techniques (WIPE-OUT 2025)** was co-located with ECML-PKDD 2025, September 15, 2025, Porto, Portugal.

As AI adoption soars, so do concerns over data privacy, ethics, and regulatory compliance. Machine Unlearning (MU) enables the selective removal of learned information without costly retraining — mitigating biases, protecting sensitive data, and aligning AI with ethical standards.

## Topics of Interest

- Theoretical foundations and guarantees for machine unlearning
- Scalable unlearning algorithms
- Privacy-preserving machine learning and the right to be forgotten
- Evaluation metrics and benchmarking for unlearning
- Applications in LLMs, computer vision, and recommender systems
